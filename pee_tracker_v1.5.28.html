⚙️ Tracking preferences

Paw steps are an estimate based on distance and a Labrador gait reference, not a sensor reading. Shoulder height adjusts the estimate approximately. Weight and sex are saved for your dog's profile but don't alter the estimate.

About Pee Tracker

A celebration of Stevie, one walk at a time.

About

Created for Stevie, our pride and joy, because we find endless fascination in everything she does — even where she chooses to pee. What began as a curiosity about her daily habits became a small tribute to a dog who brings immeasurable joy to our lives.

Development

Created by Jim Statham, with development assistance from ChatGPT.
Established November 2026

Privacy & data

Walk history and pee locations are stored locally in your browser. Regular JSON backups are recommended. External services receive requests for maps and other resources; GPS gap routing may send route coordinates to an external routing service.
